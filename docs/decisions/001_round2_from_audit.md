# Decision 001 — Round 2 follows Round 1 audit

Round 1 pair-statistics classifier exceeded the registered behavioral evidence threshold on ID and cue-shift stimuli. Repair the stimulus distribution before any RNN scientific training. Preserve the rejected generator as legacy, use 2–6 nonoverlapping disks, stratify target foreground area and raster bounding extent independently of count, and verify actual measured statistics.

Round 2 is E0 with a repaired task. Keep model, optimizer grid, seed counts and training budgets from Round 1. New banks/test streams. At most 3 exploratory seeds × 2 learning rates per model, then 5 final seeds. Audit gate: both-image nuisance classifier ≤.55 on ID and shifted held-out images (4000 balanced trials); test-only controls ≤.55. This finite classifier battery does not prove absence of every visual strategy.

Area and extent are manipulated across train vs shift, not identically fixed forever. Image construction anchors extrema, a limitation to be disclosed. Main claims limited to counts 2–6 in this stimulus family. Final H1a thresholds remain ID >.75, shift >.65, decode >.40, reset .45–.55. H1b as before. Do not choose model hyperparameters from final tests.
