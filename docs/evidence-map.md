# Evidence map

- [Ditz & Nieder 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27881787/): quantity-selective sensory and delay activity in crow NCL; tested counts 1,2,4,7,12,20,30, 800 ms sample, 1000 ms delay. Supports target phenomena, not identification of unique recurrent mechanism. Round 2 uses an explicitly synthetic 2–6 subset family, not this protocol.
- [Ditz et al. 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-022-03208-z): pooled data from three crows, 1169 recorded units, 1136 retained, waveform-inferred types. Supports comparison of tuning and putative inhibitory interactions, not direct anatomical identification or attractor proof. Raw or processed access audited separately.
- Stacho 2020 and Area X: boundaries already recorded in RESEARCH_PLAN sections 2 and 19; not used to specify the present model's graph. Branch not started; no claim of data verification.

Simulation assumptions: nonnegative rate units, 80% E units in prospective E/I model, abstract time steps, learned visual encoder, binary readout and supervised Adam training. These are not measured crow circuit parameters.
